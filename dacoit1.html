08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Flat design — elevation is expressed through color contrast and borders, not shadows.

Flat / borderless — this site uses no box-shadow elevation. Depth is created through background-color contrast and solid borders.
Border elevation — 1px solid border distinguishes surfaces. Used for cards, inputs, and containers.
Background elevation — lighter/darker fill communicates nesting, no shadow needed.
